### Question 4
Industry representatives report using a range of practices to assess engineering performance. These include:
    - **Scenario-based and case-based evaluations** that focus on reasoning, trade-offs, and problem scoping rather than correctness
    - **Oral questioning or defenses** to probe individual understanding and detect superficial contributions
    - **Process-based assessment**, including agile practices such as stand-ups, sprint reviews, and regular check-ins
    - **Decision logs and traceability artifacts** to capture reasoning and context behind technical choices
    - **Multi-source evidence**, combining code contributions, discussions, reviews, and stakeholder interactions
    - **Emphasis on visibility of work**, ensuring contributions are documented and observable
    - **De-emphasis of simplistic quantitative metrics** (e.g., lines of code, number of commits), due to their susceptibility to gaming
    - **Evaluation relative to individual level and growth trajectory**, rather than absolute performance
    - **Assessment of teamwork behaviours**, such as communication, coordination, self-organization, and stakeholder alignment
